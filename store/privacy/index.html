LiteStream Studio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

LiteStream Studio ("the app") is a desktop capture and streaming studio published by pinchezmedia254.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data to our servers. The app has no accounts, analytics, or advertising.

Screen, camera and microphone

The app can capture your screen, windows, camera and microphone only after you grant permission. Captured audio and video are processed on your device. They are sent elsewhere only when you start a stream to a destination you configure (for example an RTMP server), or save a recording to your own disk.

Local data

Scenes, sources, imported Bible editions, images and settings are stored locally on your device (in the app's browser storage) so your workspace reopens as you left it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Online Bible (optional)

If you use the optional NLT online feature, the verse references you request are sent to the NLT API (api.nlt.to), which is operated by a third party under its own terms and privacy policy. No other data is sent.

Streaming destinations

Streams you start are sent to the service you choose. That service's privacy policy applies to that content.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
